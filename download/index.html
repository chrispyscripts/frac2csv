v1.0.0

  • Trican POST-FRAC SUMMARY reports now carry a date and a start time. They previously exported neither — 0 of 39 stages on 00005, 0 of 28 on 00317 — because the chart only prints elapsed minutes. Both are now read off the STAGE INFORMATION page that follows each chart. Across all 192 Trican files, 6,657 of 6,795 stages (98%) are dated, and the derived clock runs forwards on every one of them
  • Schlumberger fluid and proppant breakdowns are exported. Each Zone and Interval Summary sheet prints a per-stage fluid table (pad, slurry, spacer, flush, sweep — or the PUMPDOWN/FRAC steps) and a per-proppant-type table with designed vs placed tonnage. Neither was read before; 4,514 fluid rows and 1,805 proppant rows across 513 sheets in testing
  • Six Liberty 2025 files that produced nothing now extract in full. Their curves are drawn as filled ribbons rather than stroked lines, which the reader skipped entirely, and two of them title their charts "STG 1" instead of "Stage 1" so were never even detected. 01103, 01104, 01105, 01106, 01397 and 01398 now give 568 of 568 chart pages and 1.94 million samples, from zero
  • Correction to values in earlier builds — please re-export affected Liberty wells. A black curve with its own printed axis was being read off a coloured axis of the same unit. Where the two disagreed the numbers were wrong, not just imprecise. Across all 12 Liberty vector files, 403 of 901 pages carried a wrong value, and every one of them is a black series — no coloured channel moved anywhere. Hydr Pressure came out roughly 4.5x too high on 182 pages, pinned near the top of the chart. PFR-ZC FR CONC came out too LOW by a factor that depends on the range printed on that page: half on 147 pages, a third on 73, a quarter on 1. Do not try to correct an old export by multiplying — the factor is not the same on every page. Re-export the affected wells. Verified against the reports' own printed axes and the ink position on the page
  • Choose which columns your CSVs carry. A new Columns… button in the Export menu lists every column for the file on screen with a checkbox each. Save as default for this filetype remembers it per service provider, so unchecking a column once on a Liberty file applies to every Liberty export after it. UWI, STAGE and DATETIME always export, so a row can always be traced back
  • The export destination folder is now actually used. Setting a destination did nothing whenever the PDF's own folder was writable — the export reported success and the folder you chose stayed empty. The export panel now also names the folder the files really went to
  • A batch of files with no data files ONE report, not one each. "Report all empty" sends a single report listing every file and its reason
  • Flagging an error survives a GitHub outage. A 503 is retried, and if it still fails the message says the service is down and your report is still there to re-send — it is no longer discarded-looking
  • The app keeps a log. Errors used to go to a console window and nowhere else. There is now a log file (its location is printed when the app starts) with the full detail of any failure, which you can send with a report
  • What is still NOT fixed, so you know what to expect from the "no extractable data" batch. This release fixes 6 of those files, not the whole set — please do not re-run all of them expecting them to come out. Of the 42 reported files we can currently open, 6 are fixed above and the rest break down as: 19 files whose charts are drawn with every label converted to line art, so there is no text on the page for the reader to find at all — the curves are perfectly good and this is the next thing being built, and it is the biggest remaining group; 8 files (01151-01158) that contain no treatment charts in the first place — they are Ovintiv WellOps daily reports, all tables plus a single wellbore schematic, so "no data" is the correct answer for those and the job is to make the app say so instead of looking like it failed; and 9 more that are a mix. Separately, 152 of the reported files are not on either drive we have — they are all 2021 and 2022 filings and are presumably on the drive still to come

v0.9.10

  • Trican concentration no longer goes blank where it sits at zero (reports #105 and #109). The blanks came in two runs per stage because that is exactly where conc rests at zero — the pad at the start and the flush at the end. The chart draws that line ON the bottom frame, the frame is painted over it, and the reader was cropping one row too high, so the only surviving green was in the row being thrown away. Checked across all 103 layout-A chart pages: 22,262 of 23,311 blank columns (95.5%) had ink sitting there the whole time. DH Prop Conc goes from 29.1% blank to 1.7%. WH Prop Conc goes from 51.2% to 41.2% — better, but not finished, and still being worked. Pressure and rate are untouched by this change: 0 peaks moved on any of them, checked rather than assumed
  • Trican's two concentration curves are separated properly (same reports). They were split by two independent colour rules with a gap between them, and 32% of the green ink matched neither rule and was dropped. It is now a clean partition with no gap. A real fix, though it turned out not to be the main cause of the blanks above
  • CalFrac stages are dated from the right summary sheet (report #98). A well prints its Multiple-Zone summary as a RUN of sheets carrying different days, and each chart was being handed the last sheet's day instead of its own. It now picks the sheet whose zone columns actually contain that chart's first zone. Checked against BCER's own filed dates over 242 wells: stages dated exactly right go from 58.2% to 71.7%, off-by-one drops from 1,598 to 1,131, and other wrong dates from 676 to 432. The number of stages matched went UP as well, 5,437 to 5,514, so nothing was quietly dropped to make the percentage look better
  • CalFrac pages that ran several stages together now split (report #101). Where the pumping data suggested more than one possible boundary, the printed zone start times are used to choose the real one
  • The Stacked window stays in step with the Lab. The link is a two-way loop now — the second window asks for the current chart when it loads, when you click back onto it, and on a slow timer. It recovers on its own after the Lab is reloaded (937 ms) and after a push that fails to arrive (103 ms), instead of leaving one chart's numbers sitting under another chart's page number
  • Full page view no longer blanks the ghost overlay. The panel and the graph's backdrop were sharing one drawing buffer, so switching to Full page wiped the ghost off the top graph. They are two separate buffers now
  • Schlumberger Zone Summary sheets frame their chart properly (report #95). These sheets were being filed with no chart geometry, so the IMAGE tag did not show and Compare Original fitted the entire sheet — tables and all — instead of the chart on it
