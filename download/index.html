New in this version

  • Trican reports now show charts. They were coming up as tables only — the curves are stored as images, which is why they were missed. 347 reports, ~12,900 charts. Checked against the maximums your own stage pages print: pressure and rate within ~1%, concentration 1–4%.
  • Sanjel reports now work — they were coming up with no data. There was no Sanjel reader, and the Canyon one was grabbing Sanjel pages and then failing on them. Against the Treatment Details table: pressure 0.14%, rate 0.24%, concentration 0.48%.
  • CalFrac slurry rate was reading ~11% low on almost every stage — the top tick on the rate axis was being dropped, so the scale was short. Against your Treatment Summary pages, stages more than 10% out went from 38% to 5%.
  • CalFrac surface concentration was missing from about a third of stages. The colour it is drawn in varies between reports and only one exact shade was recognised; it now reads each page's own legend. Coverage went from 254 pages to 652.
  • CalFrac bottom-hole concentration held the wellhead figure again on the 2013–2016 layout — purple and teal are swapped there, so both columns carried the same channel and the formation concentration was thrown away. About 3 in 4 stages affected.
  • CalFrac charts on the 2024 layout were reading a curve as the plot border, giving 600-minute charts and rates like 65 m³/min. Impossible rates went from 28 to zero.
  • Treating pressure on some STEP wells was reading 5× low — index 01396 and 01392 have an unlabelled 0–20 scale outside the real 0–100 one. Whole wells move from ~14 MPa to ~73.
  • STEP stages that came back empty now read — some pages are saved smaller than the rest of the file and their time labels were being clipped. 226 charts recovered across four wells, plus several Halliburton plots.
  • Trican tables were showing the Designed figure where the As-Pumped column was blank. 97 stages across 33 reports corrected — one showed 25.17 t of proppant where 0.17 t was pumped. Start and finish times now fill in on 1,582 pages that were blank.
  • Trican charts with a misread time axis (81-hour stages) are now dropped rather than exported. Whole-job CONTINUOUS pages are unaffected.
  • The original chart now shows behind the curves for Halliburton and Trican — it was never being drawn for either.

Worth knowing

  • Gaps in a curve come out blank now, not as a flat line. Where a chart has no ink — before a curve starts, after it ends, or across a break — the old CSV carried the last value it saw. About one reading in eight was filled in that way. Charts will show gaps where the page genuinely has none. That is the fix, not a fault.
  • The small spikes on CalFrac treating pressure are real — 85 of 92 sit in the shut-down at the end of the stage, and 99.6% of our pressure samples land on printed ink. Nothing to fix there.
