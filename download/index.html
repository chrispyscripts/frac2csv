New in this version

  • STEP treating pressure was reading 5× low on some wells — the chart stacks an unlabelled 0–20 axis outside the real 0–100 one and we were fitting the wrong one. Whole wells shift from ~14 MPa to ~73 MPa.
  • STEP stages that came back empty now extract: pages filed at a smaller render had their time labels clipped. 226 charts recovered across four wells, plus several Halliburton plots.
  • Gaps in a curve export as blank. Where a chart has no ink — before a curve starts, after it ends, or across a break — the CSV used to carry a flat line at the last value seen. About one reading in eight was filled in this way. Those are empty now, so charts show gaps where the page has none.
  • STEP rate and concentration were read off each other's axis — rate near 950 m³/min, concentration near 6 kg/m³. Fixed on 212 channels.
  • STEP traces no longer show narrow spikes dropping toward zero; those were a second curve of the same colour being read as part of the trace.
  • STEP scanned charts: pressure read off the wrong axis on some wells — one stage exported −51 MPa. 81 pages gained a pressure curve they never had.
  • CalFrac reports that draw several zones on one chart are split into a graph per zone, timed from the zone summary table.
  • Halliburton: 24 of 36 IFS reports previously came back "no extractable data"; and WH Prop Conc was missing from every Hal-1 report.
  • Chart view: the original page is always shown behind the curves — up and down arrows move through stages and roll on to the next report, left and right hide either layer. Flag Error is one Enter to open, one to send, and now states what it attached.
