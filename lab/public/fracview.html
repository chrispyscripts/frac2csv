Scroll to zoom, drag to pan — same as the Lab's graph. Click a stage to highlight it here and bring the Lab's graph and the stacked window to the same one. Double-click inside a stage to split it there — a stage labelled as a range like 1-30 numbers its pieces 1…30 as you split it. Adjust mode drags the boundary between two stages to move where one ends and the next begins. Trim mode drags out a span to cut — grey while you are dragging, and once it lands you can still drag either edge to fine-tune; click inside a cut to put it back. Fill Gaps drags out a span where the trace was LOST and bridges the holes in it with a straight line — only holes with a real reading on both sides, so a curve resting at zero or one that has not started yet is left alone. Every edit lands on the Lab's graphs and on the exported CSV.
