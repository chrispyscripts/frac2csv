**The chemical plot's channels now reach the export, and the pressures on those pages are right.** A Liberty stage page carries two charts — the Treatment Plot and, under it, the Chemical Plot — and the template reuses colours between them. v1.11.3 stopped the two red curves being drawn as one, which is what removed the comb. But everything except the ink was still filed under the colour alone, and three things were still wrong underneath it.

**A channel was missing rather than wrong.** Red is Treating Pressure on the top plot and J475 Conc on the bottom one; only the first got a name, so the chemical channel never appeared in the CSV at all — and with the comb gone there was nothing to suggest a channel was missing. Where green collides the same way, `DR42430 CONC` was absent too. Both now come through as channels of their own.

**The treating pressure was being read against both plots' ladders at once.** On 00949 stage 1 that reported 34.9 to 60.0 MPa against a chart that plainly runs 0 to 77. The same collision on 00619 put BH Prop Conc at 3.8 kg/m³ where the chart draws 409 — on every chart of that file, with nothing about the curve looking wrong. Each plot is now read against its own ladders.

**A curve drawn flat on the axis now reads zero.** There is no gridline at the bottom of a plot — the frame's own edge is that line — so the axis zero was the one label that never snapped to the geometry, and its offset tilted every fit on the page. Both proppant concentrations on 00949 stage 1 reported 12 and 23 kg/m³ through the third of the stage the chart draws flat on the axis.

**A black chemical reads its own printed axis.** Black tick labels are aligned on their left edge, so a short "0.0" sits a couple of points off the "0.200" above it — enough that Frac2CSV refused to see the column as an axis and had the channel borrow a coloured one instead. B596 Conc was being read against half its own scale on three charts of 00974 and against 0..1000 on one.

Measured over 184 chart pages in 23 Liberty files: 15 channels recovered, **no channel lost anywhere**, 12 axis ranges corrected, and a median move of 0.04% of full scale on everything else.
