**Fixes three things v1.11.54 got wrong: Surface Pressure ten times low on 8 STEP charts, Combined Clean Rate missing on 2 chemical charts, and day-long offset plots cut at an earlier stage.**

**STEP: Surface Pressure ten times low (8 charts)**
- 2026 reports: 00047 p213 and p214, 00049 p143, 00052 p156 and p157, 00054 p163 and p164. They read 7.4-9.5 MPa in v1.11.54 and now read their printed 74-95 MPa, as in v1.11.53.
- 2017 report 00273 p62 (stage 22): 2.3-5.1 MPa, now 23-51 MPa like the rest of the book.
- The cause: each axis prints 100, 80, 60, 40, 20 (or 75, 60, 45, 30, 15), and OCR also read a stray digit between two of them (a "5" near where 50 would be). v1.11.54 took it for the one label that kept its decimal point on an axis that had lost them all, and divided the axis by ten. An axis in steps of 20 prints no 50, so a label now has to fall on one of the axis's own steps. 00208's Clean Rate, the axis that repair was built for, is still repaired.
- With its pressure right, 00273 p62 now agrees with stage 23's chart where they overlap. It hands its last 5.2 minutes to stage 23, as its own chemical chart already did, and stage 23's chart starts 5 seconds earlier (01:04:20) to line up.

**STEP: Combined Clean Rate back on 2 chemical charts**
- 00050 p140 and 00054 p132 (2026 STEP). Their legends under the plot half-read: they named the SFR pens but not the clean rate, and the half-read legend replaced the chart's own list of pens. The rate pen now stays wherever the legend does not name a clean rate. 00054 p132 keeps its legend pens too (SFR-9902Z, Secure Blo 550 C, Sfr -9907).

**Day-long offset plots kept as printed**
- 00272 p49 is filed as stage 15 but plots the whole day, 02:45-15:45 (stages 7 to 15). v1.11.54 cut it where stage 7's chart opens, so stage 15 kept 2 hours and handed 10.9 hours, its own minutes among them, to stage 7. 00269 p85 (stage 29, 13:00-20:45) and 00242 p70 (stage 25's chemistry) were cut the same way.
- A chart no longer hands more than half its window to an earlier stage. These three are kept as printed and the file's notes say so. 00269 p78 (stage 26) starts at its filed 14:05:00 again.
- A backward hand-over of a few minutes is unchanged (00197 p52 still gives its last 5 minutes to stage 16).

**Still open**
- 00242 p66's chemical plot (stage 23) still hands its last 11.8 minutes to the stage 25 day plot rather than to stage 24, as in v1.11.54.

**Which templates this release touches:** STEP readings only. The hand-over rule runs on every template; across every saved read in the Lab cache (1,076 reads of 232 files) it changes only the three charts above.
