**Stacked view: filters apply while the Lab window is out of sight.**

This was the real reason the stacked view's filters did nothing, and the reason for the *Not applied* notice that v1.11.47 put up.

The Lab applied every filter change on its next screen redraw, so that a slider being dragged costs one pass per frame. Chrome does not redraw a window that cannot be seen: a background tab, or a window covered by another. While the stacked window is in use, the Lab is almost always one or the other. So the Lab held each change until it was looked at again, and the stacked window, waiting for an answer, got none.

A change from the stacked window is now applied as soon as it arrives, whether the Lab is in view or not. A burst of changes, such as a slider being dragged, is applied in one pass. Only the repaint of the Lab's own chart waits until the Lab is in view.

Reproduced with real windows in Chrome by stopping the Lab's screen redraws. On v1.11.47 the change never applied and the notice appeared. On v1.11.48 Median 37% on 00051 Stage 1's Tr Press changes 1,483 of 16,215 samples, with no notice. The earlier checks still pass: normal use, a Lab that lost track of its stacked window, a Lab reload, a stacked window from another version, and a second Lab tab.

**Which templates this release touches:** none. Every template reads exactly as it did under v1.11.47.
