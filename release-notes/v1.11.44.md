**Lab: filters per chart in the stacked view, the SG filter no longer dips below zero, and Plot by row keeps stages apart.**

**The Savitzky-Golay filter no longer goes below zero, or over a peak.** At a sharp corner, such as a pump shutdown, the smoothing curve swung past the data. A drop from 60 MPa to 0 came out at −4.2 below the floor and at 64.8 over a 60.8 hold. Each smoothed sample is now held within the readings it was fitted to. Straight runs and peaks read exactly as they did. Robust LOESS gets the same guard.

**Filters in the stacked view.**

- **The master filter** sits across the top. It is the Lab toolbar's own setting, so changing it in either window changes it in both.
- **Under each chart's title:** follow the master, or give that chart a filter of its own.
- **Pick area:** drag across the chart to hold its filter to that stretch. The rest of the chart stays as read, and the stretch is shaded on the chart.
- **Save for this stage** keeps all of it, the same as the Lab's Save ▾ › Save for this chart only.

The Lab's chart and the export read the same filtered curves. Applied to every stage in a file, or saved as a provider default, the per-chart settings go without their areas, because an area is a stretch of one particular stage.

**Plot by row: two stages whose clocks overlap are no longer drawn on top of each other.** The CSV writes each stage's samples as a block of rows of its own. Plot by row placed every sample by its clock instead, so where two charts share minutes, the second was drawn over the first and labelled with the first one's row numbers. On 00051, each stage's two charts share up to 276 minutes. Each block now sits on its own rows, and the row under the cursor is the row in the file.

**Which templates this release touches:** none. Every template reads exactly as it did under v1.11.43. Only the Lab's filters, its stacked window and FracView change. An exported curve changes only where a Savitzky-Golay or LOESS filter is on and had overshot.
