**Two new tools on the menu, each opening in its own window: Directional Survey and Geology.**

**Directional Survey**
- Drop a survey report PDF, or a CSV / TXT table of stations. The tool reads each station's MD, inclination and azimuth, plus the report's own TVD, N/S and E/W where it gives them, and the well's UWI.
- The path is worked out again by **minimum curvature**, then shown before you save: a **plan view** from above (E/W across, N/S up) and a **section view** from the side (TVD down, along the closure azimuth).
- **Save as CSV:** UWI, MD (m), Inc (deg), Azi (deg), TVD (m), N/S (m), E/W (m), DLS (deg/30 m), VS (m). Tables in feet are converted to metres.
- When a report gives TVD, the tool shows how far its own TVD is from the report's.
- On 344 real survey reports, 341 were read. The tool's TVD matched the report's to a median of 2 mm and was never more than 0.3 m off. The other 3 PDFs have text in a font with no readable characters, and the tool says so: there is no OCR.

**Geology**
- Drop a geologist's report PDF. The tool reads the **Sample Descriptions** section and its **formation tops**. When the section has no tops, it uses the report's formation tops table.
- Each depth interval gets the formation it lies in, shown as "A / B" when a top falls inside the interval. Points that aren't formations (KOP, landing point, TD and so on) are kept apart as markers.
- **Save as CSV:** UWI, Depth Range, Formation, Description, with **the description always in quotes**. A second CSV holds the formation tops: UWI, Formation, MD, TVD, SS.
- All 350 real geologist's reports were read: 11,173 sample intervals, and 349 reports had tops.

**LAS extraction: printouts stitched into one page**
- A printout stitched into a single tall page (a "-Stitch" copy) now reads its curves as well as its paged copy does. Before, the strip around a one-page log was made about three logs long. The log's own lines were then too short to be found as its track edges, and most curves were lost.
- Paged printouts and WellSight logs read exactly as before.

**Checked**

- Both tools were run over the full set of real survey and geologist's reports, as above.
