**Stacked view: filter changes reach the Lab, and when one can't, the stacked window says so.**

Changing a filter in the stacked window can still do nothing on one combination of windows. It happens when the Lab window has been reloaded, or has another stacked window open, and no longer counts that stacked window as its own. The Lab then ignored every change from it without a word. The chart did not change, the export did not change, and nothing on screen said why.

- **The Lab takes a filter change from whichever stacked window is showing the chart the Lab is showing,** and adopts that window as its stacked window from then on.
- **The stacked window shows its version beside its title** and checks it against the Lab's. If the stacked window is out of date, it reloads itself once. If the Lab tab is the one out of date, the stacked window says to reload it.
- **A change the Lab does not answer within two seconds** is retried once over a fresh link. If it is still not answered, the filter bar shows a red notice: *Not applied — the Lab window is not answering this one. Reload the Lab tab, then press Stacked there.*

Tested with real windows in Chrome: normal use, a Lab that had lost track of its stacked window, a link to nowhere, and a stacked window from another version.

**Which templates this release touches:** none. Every template reads exactly as it did under v1.11.46. Only the link between the Lab and its stacked window changes.
