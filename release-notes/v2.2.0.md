**WellSight logs: curves that run past their printed scale reported, the log's header in a window of its own, and each data set over its own column of the printout.**

**Curves past their printed scale**
- Each curve's printed scale is now read from the log, as LogViewer prints it, including a scale that changes partway down the log (gas on 0 to 500, then 0 to 4000 from 1,410 m).
- LogViewer wraps a reading that runs past its scale back in from the other edge of the track, once, in a thin line: gas at 1,074 on a 0 to 1000 scale is printed at 74. A reading past twice the scale stays at the edge and cannot be read off the printout.
- Frac2CSV now says where this happens:
  - **↻** in the readings, with where the printout draws the reading;
  - a band along the top of the curve chart over the wrapped depths, and the scale's end as a dashed line;
  - a line per curve in the notes.
- The readings shown and saved are always the file's own, not the printout's wrapped line.
- **Save LAS** also writes `printed-scales` and `wraps` CSVs.
- Checked against the drawn lines on about 300 of the drive's printouts: the scales match for 87 to 89% of curves, and 99% of readings past the scale once are drawn where one wrap puts them.

**View header**
- A **View header ↗** button beside the log's title opens its header in a separate window that follows the Lab as you go through the logs.
- The window shows the header page's fields, the boxes (Operator, Geologist, Drilling, Directional, Comments), a custom header page as typed, and the printout's own header and legend pages.
- For a log with no typed header, it shows the pictures the log holds; some may be other wells' headers.
- **↑ ↓** in the header window step through the logs. **Copy** puts the fields on the clipboard.

**Each data set over its own column of the printout**
- The data sets in the middle column now switch on and off, so the right side can show several at once, up to all of them.
- Each data set is a card: its chart, with the printout's own column for it underneath (Curves over the curve track, Oil shows over the oil-show column, and so on). The cards stay in their pairs as you scroll.
- **↑ ↓** go to the next log with the same data sets on.
- The columns are placed from the log's own layout. On 414 of the drive's printouts, 96.5% of column edges land on the printout's lines.
- One drop box takes PDFs, WellSight logs and CSV files; the PDF Reports / CSV Files switch is gone.

**Fixed**
- Hovering over a chart no longer makes the Lab shake or the stacked window zoom in and out.

**Behind the scenes:** groundwork for reading logs from their PDFs alone: a vocabulary of the names logs use for curves, units, tracks and header fields, and an answer key from the drive's logs and printouts to check a PDF reader against. The app works as before.

**Checked:**
- 1,629 Python tests and the JS tests pass, run with the WellSight drive.
- PDF reading is unchanged from v2.1.0.
