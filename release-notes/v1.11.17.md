**The continuous chart is gone, and so is the notice offering to show it.**

A CONTINUOUS chart is the whole job re-plotted end to end with no stage number. It was still arriving as "Stage ?" at the end of the list — 1,180 minutes and 70,800 rows against real stages of 25 to 105 minutes — and it is the source of the big green proppant spike: that page draws the whole job in the width a stage page gives one stage, so a pixel is about a minute, and its proppant reads 1,240 kg/m³ where the stage charts say 170.

Three releases tried to hold it back in the app and it kept coming through. So it is now dropped by the reader instead, before anything downstream ever sees it. There is nothing left to filter, nothing to remember, and no "Show" notice, because it is not sent at all. On the file we have been comparing: 28 stages, no nameless band, no spike, no notice.

Whatever it alone covered is still reported in the extraction notes, so nothing goes quiet.

**The Lab page is also no longer cached by the browser.** It was served with no cache header, so a browser could hold on to an old copy of it — while the version number beside the wordmark still read the new one, because that is fetched live. That combination meant a fix could ship, the app could say it was the new version, and the page could still be running the old script. That is fixed, but it does mean **one hard reload after updating** is worth doing this time.
