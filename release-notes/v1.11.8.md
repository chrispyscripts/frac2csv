**The red comb on Liberty pressure is fixed — and it was never just stage 1.**

Every Liberty stage page prints two plots: the Treatment Plot on top and a Chemicals Plot underneath. Both draw a curve in pure red — Treating Pressure above, J475 Conc below — and we were grouping a page's curves by colour across the whole page. So the chemical curve was being spliced into the pressure. On the file you sent there are 36 red paths where every other colour has exactly 18, and red is the only colour appearing in both plots.

The chemical curve sits well below the pressure frame, so it clips to the bottom of the pressure scale, and the reading jumps between a real pressure and roughly zero once per slice. That is the comb, with the smooth black ghost running across the top of it.

Curves are now tied to the plot their own legend entry belongs to. On 00949, the share of Treating Pressure samples jumping more than a quarter of the full range:

- stage 1: 56.7% → 0%
- stage 2: 83.5% → 0%
- stage 3: 37.9% → 0%
- stage 4: 44.9% → 0%

Stage 2 was the worst of them. All seven reports came in against stage 1 because stage 1 is the one you open first. Pressure now reads 34.9 to 60.0 MPa instead of 0 to 59.5, and every other channel is unchanged. All six files you reported come back clean.

**Continuous re-plots no longer arrive as stages.** A CONTINUOUS chart is the whole job drawn end to end with no stage number, and the stage charts already hold the same minutes at much higher resolution. We drop one when we can prove the stages cover it, which needs a clock — and these do not always print one. Checking fourteen Trican files, one of them keeps fifteen such charts, nine to twenty hours each, all arriving as nameless numbered stages in a file whose real stages run about two hours.

They are now kept out of the stage list and out of the CSV. Nothing is thrown away: a line under the stage list says how many there are and Show puts them back.
