**STEP: the leftover dips from 1.11.39 (#799).**

1.11.39 fixed the column of a steep rise that read back at the bottom. On some rises, though, the next column still read the bottom of the thick stroke, leaving a smaller dip: 00048 p130's first rise read 20, 21.5, then 7 MPa.

Right after a repaired column, a reading that falls back is now repaired too, while the edge of the stroke it sits on is still closing fast on the new level. A notch actually drawn just after a rise holds its edge, and a gentle climb's edge creeps, so both keep their readings. A repair like this reads no further on than the next two columns, so a slanted rise gains no peak. 00048 p130 now climbs 20, 21.5, 20, 25.6, 32.7, 36. Sharp falls get the same treatment, and many shutdown falls that jumped back up for a column now go straight down.
