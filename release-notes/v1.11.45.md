**Stacked view: as many filter areas per chart as you need, with edges you can drag, and every filter now shows what it changed.**

**Areas.**

- **+ Add area** under a chart's title, then drag across the chart. Each area gets a filter of its own: the master, **None** (leave that stretch exactly as read, such as a real spike), or any of the filters.
- **Add as many as you like.** Where two overlap, the later one wins.
- **Drag an area's edges** on the chart to move them. Each edge has a handle.
- **Delete an area** with the × on its chip, or click inside it and press Delete.
- **Outside the areas** the chart reads through its own setting: the master, or a filter of its own.

**Seeing what a filter did.** On 00051's Stage 11, a Hampel filter at 100% on the opening climb looked as if it was not applied. It was applied, but it changed 5 of 258 samples, by at most 6 MPa. Hampel only removes a sample that stands apart from its neighbours, and on a steep climb few do. To make that visible:

- The curve **as read is drawn faintly behind** any filtered chart. Where the grey shows, that is what the filter took out. Where there is one line, the filter left the curve alone.
- Each chart and each area says **how many samples it changed and by how much**. For example, "changed 673 of 1262 samples · max 0.21 MPa", or "changes nothing here".

This also shows when a filter takes out too much. A Robust LOESS master at 10% on Stage 11 rounds the shutdown off entirely, by up to 69 MPa.

A setting saved in v1.11.44, with a single stretch, keeps its meaning: that stretch through the chart's filter, and the rest of the chart as read.

**Which templates this release touches:** none. Every template reads exactly as it did under v1.11.44. Only the Lab's stacked window and its filters change.
