**Trican's 2024&ndash;2025 reports give up the curves that were hidden under other curves.** On those reports a curve that runs underneath another is not drawn at all where the two meet, and until now only one pair of the nine was ever recovered &mdash; wellhead concentration from under downhole concentration, and only ever in that direction. Monitor Pressure and WH Slurry Rate were never recovered at all. Measured over 27 chart pages in nine filings, Monitor Pressure goes blank on nine of them for up to 45% of the time it is drawn, and WH Slurry Rate on four for up to 72%. All of it now comes back, every pair is walked, and which of the two concentration curves is underneath is read off that chart's own pixels rather than assumed. On one page 348 seconds of Monitor Pressure and 574 of WH Slurry Rate came back that the export used to leave empty. Every recovered stretch is flagged as recovered, so Isolate Interpolation shows them and they never pass for ink the page actually carries.

**A stage whose hour labels are misread now reads the same way every time.** This report's font prints a 9 that reads as a 3, so a chart's clock can come back six hours out. Where the misread is the majority the ladder alone cannot say which hour is true, and the app was settling it by an accident of ordering &mdash; the same chart could be read 09:21 on one machine and 03:21 on another. It is settled on the font now: a 9 reads as 3 and never the other way round, so that is the direction that gets put back, and a Start Time the page prints still overrules it.
