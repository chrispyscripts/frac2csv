**Frac2CSV now reads WellSight logs.** Drop .sl6, .slg, .hl6 or .hlg files on the upload panel, next to PDFs, and each opens as a log: its curves as LAS, CSV and JSON, its lithology, and its LogViewer printout under the curves to check them against.

**WellSight logs (desktop app)**
- Each log's header (well, licence, UWI), depth range and depth axis (MD or TVD), every curve on a 0.2 m step, and the lithology intervals.
- **Save LAS** writes the LAS, CSV and JSON beside your other exports.
- With a log selected, the middle column lists its data sets (Curve Track for now) and the right shows the curves with depth across: zoom, drag, hover for values, double-click for the whole log.
- **Flag Error** works on a log as it does on a PDF chart.

**The printout as a reference**
- LogViewer prints a log over many Letter pages. Frac2CSV places each page by its own printed depth labels, turns it, and stitches the pages into one long strip on the log's depth scale, so the printout sits straight under the LAS curves.
- For now only the printout's **curve chart** is shown: the descriptions, lithology and cross-section tracks are left out. **Open PDF** opens the stitched curve chart; Save writes it as `<log>.printout-curves.pdf` and the whole stitched printout beside it.
- **Printouts folder:** Settings › WellSight printouts (or **Printouts folder…** on a log) names the folder that holds the printouts. Every PDF under it is indexed once and a dropped log finds its printout by name. The folder is chosen in Frac2CSV's own folder browser: drives, then folders, then Use this folder. With none set, a `SK-VIKING\WellSight` folder at the top of any drive is used.
- A printout PDF dropped together with its log, under the same name, is used too.
- **Print with LogViewer:** on a PC with WellSight's free LogViewer installed, prints the log to PDF and stitches it. LogViewer is not included. This is new and not yet tried on a real PC; flag it if it fails.

**Stacked view**
- **Stacked ↗** opens the log in its own window, depth across: each curve in its own band (**Separated data**) or all on one chart (**Combined**), then lithology, then the printed curve chart, all on one depth scale.
- Fit whole log, a zoom slider to 1:100, and 1:240 as printed. ↑ ↓ step through the logs together with the main window; ← → move along the log.
- Zooming no longer greys the printout out: a picture of the whole printout is drawn underneath while the sharp pieces load.
