**Halliburton image charts: the wellhead proppant concentration comes back.** Wellhead and bottom-hole concentration are drawn on the same 0–1500 scale and, once proppant reaches the perfs, at very nearly the same number — so the two pens lie on top of each other and the page shows one line. The wellhead curve was the one underneath, and where it was covered it simply went missing: on one 24-chart file, 13,772 samples in 205 separate stretches, none of them short, all of them in the middle of the job at around 490 kg/m³. It is now read from under the curve covering it — a curve with no ink inside its own drawn span has to be under something, and the only thing it can be under is the other pen. **Measured over 72 charts in two files: wellhead concentration went from 61.5% to 69.5% present on one and from 68.9% to 82.5% on the other, every chart improved, and treating pressure, slurry rate and bottom-hole concentration did not move at all.** Recovered stretches are marked as deduced on the chart rather than passed off as traced ink.

**A filter default now belongs to the chart type, not the company.** Halliburton files through two completely different plotters — the IFS charts are read from the PDF's own line geometry, the treatment plots are traced from a picture — and both sat in one "Halliburton" bucket, as did STEP's three readers and CalFrac's two. A vector curve carries no digitising noise at all, so a setting dialled in on the traced charts was being applied to the vector ones where it could only take something away. Defaults are keyed on the chart type itself now. **Anything you have already saved keeps working** and narrows to the chart type the next time you save it.

**Clear a filter for one chart, or for a whole well.** "Apply to every stage in this file" had no way back: the only way to lift it was to apply "off" everywhere, which is not the same thing — that writes an explicit off onto every chart and then overrides the very default they should fall back to. Two new items in the Save menu clear a chart's own setting, or every chart's in the well, handing them back to the default. Each appears only when there is something for it to clear. Clearing a well leaves the provider default alone.

**Ten more units of reach was v1.11.29; this adds the way back out of it.**

**Export a chart whole, however the clock reads.** A chart that runs on into the next stage is cut where the next chart begins, because those minutes are re-plotted there and writing them twice would double-count them. There is now a button on the hand-over note to take the chart exactly as the page draws it. It says what it costs: those minutes then appear twice, once here and once on the stage they were handed to.

**Drop CSVs instead of PDFs.** A toggle at the top of the file column switches it between PDF reports and CSV files, and in CSV mode it takes CSVs only — anything else is turned away by name rather than quietly ignored. A dropped CSV behaves like any other table: it opens in the Tables view, every cell takes a hand edit, edits survive a re-read, and it exports the same way. Commas inside quoted cells are read properly, so a well name or a note like "screened out, 2.1 min" no longer shifts every column after it.

**Which templates this release touches:** the **Halliburton treatment plot (raster)** reader, and the Lab. Every other template reads exactly as it did under v1.11.29.
