**STEP: pressure climbs no longer zigzag (#799), and a chart that rises off the floor starts from 0 (#808).**

On 00051's Stage 6 (page 155) the treating pressure climbs from 30 to 76 MPa in about two minutes. Between 3:00 and 5:00 it read 29, 30, 41, 40, 33, 48, 55, 41, 40, 48, 49, 52, 61, 63, 64, 72 MPa: four drops of 7 to 15 MPa, each just before the pressure went on up. It now reads 33, 36, 42, 45, 45, 53, 59, 60, 64, 69, 70, 72, 74, 75, 75, 76.

The climbing stroke is drawn thick and steep, so a column cuts it top to bottom. Where the reader took the stroke's lower edge, it dropped back to where the climb had been a few seconds earlier. Those readings now follow the front of the climb. A reading stays where it is when:

- the pressure really did fall at that point on the page (00048 p142, 00051 p199 and 00054 p159 keep their V),
- the drop is more than two or three readings long, or
- nothing next to it is already on the climb.
