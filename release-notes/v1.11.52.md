**STEP stage numbers put right, 00347 and 00935 read through, offset charts read full height, and CalFrac "Zones 2" captions.**

**A v1.11.49 fault: offset charts filed one stage on.** The 2017 Shell books print two pages per stage, a treatment chart and an offset chart under the same number. v1.11.49 added a rule that numbers a page by its place in the book, and it assumed one page per stage. It took each offset page's number, which had read correctly, as out of order and moved the chart one stage on:
- 00185: eight charts, starting with p47 "Treatment 11", which was filed as stage 12;
- 00242, 00252, 00270 and 00272: one chart each.

The rule now steps aside in books that print two pages per stage. It never overrides a number the page printed when the page also prints its interval. Where one stage is printed twice and another not at all, the page count could still add up and a run of charts was shifted (00119 p144-p146); that is fixed too.

**Stage numbers from the printed interval.** Where a chart's stage number did not read, or read plainly wrong, it is now numbered by its printed interval, at the spacing the book's own numbered stages keep:
- 00347 (#811): every chart is numbered, stages 1 to 30. p93 and p94 had read "1"; they are stages 18 and 19.
- 00119: p146 "1" is stage 12, and p156 "4" is stage 22.
- 00246: "138" and "338" are stages 18 and 38.

A chart is numbered this way only when its interval falls within 0.15 of a stage, and only where the spacing is steady. A number that read is changed only when it is two or more stages from its interval; a one-stage difference is left as printed.

**00347 (#811): three pages that were passed over now read.** p99, p100 and p103 (stages 23, 24 and 27) title themselves "Interval Summary" over "Treatment N", and the OCR lost the number. They were taken for no chart.

**Offset charts read full height (00241, 00243, 00269-00274).** Some charts print their frame's side rules in pieces a few pixels apart. The frame was taken from one piece only. Some charts came out short, and a well above the lower third of the chart read blank. Others were not read at all. The frame now follows its own side rules to their full length:
- 00269 reads 108 charts where it read 82;
- 00241 reads 95 where it read 83;
- 00243 reads 109 where it read 101;
- 00270 reads 2 more and 00272 3 more;
- 00182's slurry rate now reads on p188 and p216, and still reads on p218. Once its frame reached them, the rate labels lost their decimal points (2.5 read as 25). Where the rate column would otherwise be refused, they are now read at a tenth.

Most of the new charts are offset charts with every well on its own axis. The rest are chemical charts and treatment charts that had not been found.

**Fleet charts and an unread well legend no longer read as Prop Conc (00185).** This dual-fuel book prints two other charts beside each stage's treatment and chemical charts:
- a fleet chart ("DGB Analysis": pump units, fuel rate, diesel substitution);
- an offset chart whose well legend does not read.

Their curves were named after treatment channels or chemical additives. The fleet's substitution was filed as Prop Conc, beside the stage's real one. These curves now keep their colour names. On the offset chart, the surface pressure keeps its name. Still open: two of its "Offset Analysis" pages reprint the stage's chemical chart under that header (p33, p45). Their curves are still read with treatment names.

**CalFrac "Zones 2" (00222, 00223).** A caption naming one zone in the plural, such as "Zones 1 Flush" or "Zones 2", was not read, and the chart took the zone of the chart before it. 00223 came out as 19 (2), 1 (2) and 1 (3), and 00222's zone 19 as 18 (2). A range, "Zones 1-3", is still split as before.
