**STEP: Carmine's flags #809-#829. Offset charts read, misfiled charts put back, three filings that read nothing now read, and the stacked view fits the screen.**

**Offset charts (#819, #823, #826).** Beside every stage's treatment chart, the 2017 Shell books print an offset chart. It shows the stage's surface pressure and the neighbouring wells' pressures.
- On 00243-00245 and 00269-00274 only the surface pressure was read. Every well is now read against its own axis and named from the chart's own legend: "A Well Offset Pressure" on the offset axis, "D Well ABD Pressure" on the abandoned wells' axis. That includes the pale pink and pale green wells. On 00269-00274 some offset charts are framed short at the top, so a well above about 57 MPa on the main axis comes out blank there rather than wrong.
- On 00248 the offset wells went out under treatment names: F Well's casing pressure as "Prop Conc", E Well's as "Slurry Rate", and C and O Wells as unnamed series. They were then spliced into the stage's real curves of those names. They now go out as "F Well Casing Pressure" and so on, and the stage's own curves are left alone.
- A one-letter well name is hard to read on some pages. A book prints the same legend on every stage, so each well keeps the letter the whole file reads most often for its colour.
- On 00248 these axes print every colour's tick labels stacked at each gridline, so each colour's numbers sit up to 30 px off their line. Surface Pressure read 3 MPa low and its axis came out as 75..-5. Where the printed end labels plainly mark the frame, the axis is now put on them: 0..80, 0..30.

**Slurry rate on 0..5400 (#826, #827).** On 00248 stages 35 and 46 the rate labels 8.000 and 4.000 were read as 8000 and 4000, and the fitted axis ran downward. A value axis never runs downward, so that fit is now refused and the lost decimals are restored: 3.5 and 4.6 m3/min on 0..16. The magenta curve beside it is now named "Coil Fluid Rate".

**Charts filed under the wrong stage (#819, #823).** Where OCR read "13" as "3", seven intervals' charts landed under one stage number. Stage 12 then showed only its offset chart ("missing curves"), and 00248 stage 1's treatment chart sat under stage 5 ("time issues"). Each chart's interval is printed as text, so charts are now put back by depth: from the Daily Stage Summary where there is one, otherwise from the stage whose own charts print that interval. On books whose stage numbers OCR poorly, a chart with no readable number takes its place between two numbered pages, when they are exactly that many stages apart.

**00244 (#814-#817, #822):**
- The in-plot legend swatch no longer reads as a spike.
- A neighbouring pen's edge no longer holds a curve up after it ends.
- The yellow coil pressure no longer reads as Btm Prop Conc after the job. Only the yellow line itself and its blend with the red are set aside; a real Btm Prop Conc drawn along a yellow line is kept.
- A long gap between a curve resting on the floor and the curve pumping runs along the floor, not as one long slant.
- Where Btm Prop Conc is drawn over Prop Conc, Prop Conc holds the level the two share and steps where the page steps, instead of slanting from one step to the next.

**01289 (#829):**
- Rate and concentration are read on the 2025 two-ladder axis.
- Treating pressure is read through a dip it falls into and climbs out of, instead of bridged straight across.

**Filings that read nothing:**
- **00745, 00746, 00747 (#809).** The text layer is Calibri glyph numbers, not letters. Those pages are now OCR'd: 24, 19 and 24 stages.
- **00935 (#810).** Pages are printed as pictures only. 15 charts now read, in place of none.
- **00347-00351 (#811).** Pages are drawn in an unreadable font, or as outlines. These are now OCR'd: 56 charts on 00347 and 35 stages on 00349. A stage number the OCR misses is taken from the page's place between numbered pages.
- **00570 (#813).** The 2018 Encana PRC plots name no interval. Each is now numbered from the "Interval N Summary" sheet before it: 73 intervals. The date is not printed anywhere on those pages and stays for the user to supply.

**Stacked view: charts fit the window (#829).** A new "Height" menu offers Fit window, the default, plus small, medium and large. Fit window sizes the charts so every one is in view at once, down to 110 px each. Large is the old 384 px.

**Which templates this release touches:** STEP (raster), and the Schlumberger PRC plot for unnumbered intervals. Every other template reads exactly as it did under v1.11.48.

Checked on 32 STEP files against v1.11.43: 6,390 of 9,517 channels read exactly as before. Of the rest:
- **Prop Conc** reads more of its own curve on 1,672 charts. Where Btm Prop Conc is drawn over it, it now holds the shared level instead of a gap.
- **The chemical chart's Combined Clean Rate** is read on its own 0..20 axis. On 00095 and 00100 it had been read on the 0..4 additive axis: 2 m3/min where the page draws 10 to 13.
- **Btm Prop Conc** loses readings of the yellow and red pressure lines. On 00163, 00182, 00227 and 00420 these had made peaks of 650 to 740 kg/m3 before any sand was pumped.
- **00053 stage 200's rate axis** had been fitted upside down, at 14.6..18.2. It now reads 0..20, at 10 m3/min.
- **102 channels are new**, mostly chemical-chart additives and rates that were not read before.
- **21 channels are gone.** All are a "Surface Pressure" on a chemical chart, which has none.
