**STEP: the rebound in the middle of a fall (#799).**

On 00051's Chart 2 (page 150) the pressure falls from about 72 to 44 MPa at 13:46. One column of that fall still read the top of the thick line instead of the bottom, so the export showed a spike back up to 61.6 MPa in the middle of the fall. The repair only counted a reading as fallen back if it sat in the first quarter of the move, and this one sat 26% of the way down.

It now counts the first third, but only where the move then lands where it had already got, and only right after it got there. A real V that climbs again from lower down, like 00051 p199's, keeps its bottom. So does a noisy chemical band thick enough to look like one long move. 00048 p130's first rise also reaches its top on the column the page draws it.
