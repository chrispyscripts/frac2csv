**FracView's stage boundaries and the greyed cutoff now agree, and nothing is exported twice.**

v1.11.12 stopped stages being cut before they finished pumping — stage 1 keeps its tail-in, flush and shutdown instead of handing them to stage 2. That was right, but it was only half the change: the next stage's start was left where it was. So stage 1 ran to 01:31 while stage 2 still began at 01:22, and those nine and a half minutes were exported under **both** stages.

On the file we have been comparing that was 242 minutes of the job coming out twice across 28 stages — the same duplication that was reported and fixed long ago, reintroduced at the other end of the chart. It also put FracView's band boundary in a different place from the greyed cutoff on the Lab's chart, which is how you spotted it.

The next stage now gives those minutes up: its chart opened early and re-plotted the previous stage's ending as its own lead-in, so it drops that lead-in and starts where the previous stage stops. All 28 stages now run end to end — **0.0 minutes of overlap and 0.0 minutes of gap** — and both windows draw the boundary in the same place.

Each stage says so in its own notes: one says the next chart opened before it finished, the other says its first minutes belong to the stage before it.
