**Curves read from a mudlog printout PDF come out far closer to the log's own LAS, and the app opens on a menu of its two tools.**

**Opening menu**
- The app opens on a menu of its two tools:
  - **Frac2CSV** takes frac report PDFs, a .txt drive list or CSV files, as before.
  - **LAS extraction** takes WellSight logs or a log's printout PDF.
- A printout PDF dropped on LAS extraction is read as the log straight away. When it is not a log's printout, the app says why.
- Each tool lists only its own files and reopens on the file it last had open. **Menu** at the top left goes back, and Escape on the menu returns to the open tool.
- A WellSight log dropped on Frac2CSV opens in LAS extraction.

**Curves read from a printout PDF**
- **Depths:** each reading is put back at the depth the log records it at. LogViewer draws a reading half its step shallower; the reader now undoes this for curves recorded every 0.1, 0.2, 0.5 or 1 m.
- **Held readings:** each reading is held over its recorded step, as the log's own LAS holds it.
- **Wraps:** a curve past its scale, drawn again from the other edge, is followed much more reliably. That includes junk readings, nulls, dashed lines and spikes past the top.
- **Log scales:** resistivity scales are read as logarithmic, including those printed with a single decade number.
- **Captions:** notes and depth labels are no longer taken for curve captions. The reader also says which captioned curves it could not read, and why.

**Accuracy**
- On 3,750 logs that were never used to build the reader, 98.4% of the readings read off the printout are within 2% of the scale's width of the log's own LAS.
- On the 302 printouts the reader was built on, curves score 0.97 against the answer key, up from 0.88 in v2.3.1.

**Also in this release**
- **Notes:** a printout's notes are put back together as printed. Before, notes on a lateral ran together, and lines cut by a page join stayed split. These notes feed the descriptions the app shows for a log read from its PDF.
- **WellSight printed scales:** a curve whose printed scale has a single section now reads its left end correctly. Before, it took the log's top depth instead.

**Known weak spots**
- A handful of curves can still read a scale width off where the line wraps on a narrow scale, or where it comes back after junk readings or a long gap.
- Some logs are recorded every 0.01 m. A printout read on 0.1 m rows cannot follow changes within 0.1 m.

**Checked**
- The Python tests pass, including the tests that need the WellSight drive, and so do the Lab's JS tests.
- Curve reading was checked on the 3,750 + 302 logs above.
- Not yet tested on Windows itself.
