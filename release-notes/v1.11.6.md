**The hand-over was never being drawn. Now it is.**

Where a chart runs on past its own stage, those extra minutes are on the printed page and should be on the screen, greyed, past the end of the export. Three releases in a row said that was working. It was not.

The tail was read off the page correctly and sent to the screen correctly, and then dropped on the last step before the chart — the list of stages you click. It drew only when a stage was handed straight to the chart, which is how it was tested. It never drew when a file was opened the way everyone opens one: drop it in, Extract, click a stage.

So every truncation report since v1.11.3 was right. The chart really did stop early, and the grey minutes that were supposed to explain why were not there to see.

On a 29-stage Trican file, all 29 stages came back with nothing drawn. The same file now draws the hand-over on 27 of 29 — stage 1's axis runs to 107 minutes against the 96 the export covers, and inside that shaded band you can watch the pressure fall from 60.3 to 27.4 MPa and the rate drop from 8.4 to 2.0. That is the shutdown, it is printed on the page, and it is now on your screen.

Nothing changed in the data or in the CSV. The export still ends where the stage ends — those minutes belong to the next stage and are exported there. This is about what the chart shows you.
