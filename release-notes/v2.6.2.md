**LAS extraction reads more geosteering printouts, and their gas.**

**Printouts captioned with rows of numbers** (a black caption such as "Gamma Ray (AAPI)", its scale a row of numbers in the curve's colour)
- These are now read as geosteering printouts even when the WellSight reader finds a caption of its own. Read the WellSight way, a curve on them took another curve's numbers for its scale (a gamma on the drill rate's 0–15) and stray words became curves ("mW", "SubSea").
- A caption whose name has brackets of its own is read: **"TG (Total Gas) (units)"**. Before, gas captioned this way was missed.
- Two numbers of a scale printed as one word ("12001500" for 1200 and 1500) are split where both halves keep to the scale, so the curve keeps its scale.
- A caption printed but never drawn (no line of its colour on any page) is no longer given another curve's line.

**Depths on the page**
- On a page where a cross-section's TVD scale has more labels than the MD line (and its values fall in the log's range), the MD labels are used.
- A page with only three or four MD labels (the log's first or last page) is read on them when they are on the other pages' scale; before, it was skipped.

**What the WellSight reader reads is unchanged**: WellSight printouts read exactly as before.

**Checked**
- On a set of real printouts: of 225 TRI printouts, 224 now give gas (106 before), 222 gamma (169) and 224 drill rate (183); GEO printouts that read nothing now read their curves; 407 WellSight printouts read the same as before but for one that had read stray words and now reads gamma, gas and ROP. Readings were drawn back on their pages and checked by eye.
- The Python tests pass (1,855, with the WellSight drive plugged in), and so do the Lab's JS tests.
- Not yet tested on Windows itself.
