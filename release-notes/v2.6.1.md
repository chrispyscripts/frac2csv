**Fixes to the Directional Survey and Geology tools, and LAS extraction from more kinds of printout.**

**Directional Survey**
- **Scanned reports and unreadable fonts:** when a report has no text, or its text is in a font with no readable characters, the tool now reads it by OCR. Where OCR leaves the minus sign off a report's N/S or E/W offset, it is put back when the worked-out path agrees with the value but for the sign, and the tool says so.
- **A sidetrack kept apart:** a report holding an original hole and its sidetrack is no longer merged into one path. The wellbore with the most stations is read, its UWI taken from its own label, and the tool warns that the report holds another.
- On the 344 real survey reports, all 344 now read (341 in v2.6.0); 2 now have the right UWI.

**Geology**
- **The CSV is UWI, Depth Range, "Description"**: no formation column, and the description is always in quotes. The window shows the depth range and description of each interval.
- Reports whose formation tops are in a "Geological Markers" table, or whose columns are spelled out (Measured, Vertical, Sea), are read.

**LAS extraction from printouts**
- **Geosteering printouts:** curves are now read from printouts whose captions sit at the edge of the curves' band ("150 Gamma (API)" with its other end across from it), or as a row of numbers on the header page. Gas drawn again from the band's low end when it runs past the top is put back together. On the test set, all 231 GEO and 143 of 145 TRI printouts now give curves (none did before).
- **One-page and long-page printouts:** the depth line is the row with the most depth labels, and the depth scale is the one most labels are on, so a track's own scale or a header page no longer takes its place. 153 stitched printouts that read "no depth labels found" now read.
- A track scale with no width no longer stops the reading.

**Checked**
- The Python tests pass (1,849; the 70 that need the WellSight drive were skipped), and so do the Lab's JS tests.
- Checked on a set of about 3,200 real printouts and on the full sets of real survey and geologist's reports. The regression pass on the WellSight drive was **not** run for this release.
- Not yet tested on Windows itself.
