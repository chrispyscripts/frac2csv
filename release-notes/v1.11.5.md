**The hand-over is now shaded, not just dashed.** Where a chart runs on past its own stage, those minutes are drawn past the end of the export — they are on the printed page, so they are on the chart. Until now they were light grey dashes, which on a small panel is easy to read as the chart simply stopping. The span now sits on a shaded band as well, so it is obvious at any size that the chart continues and that part of it belongs to the next stage.

**Flag Error now records what the chart was showing.** A report carries the hand-over size, how many curves were drawn into it, the length of the time axis and the length of the export. A screenshot of a small panel cannot answer whether eleven minutes of grey were drawn at its right-hand edge; these four numbers can.
