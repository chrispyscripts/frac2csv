**FracView was still showing the continuous chart as a 29th stage (#690).**

Since v1.11.8 the Lab keeps a CONTINUOUS re-plot — the whole job drawn end to end with no stage number — out of the stage list and out of the CSV. FracView was never told. It built its own view from the raw read, so on 00218 it opened with "29 stages extracted" against the Lab's 28, a band captioned "?", and a banner saying the clock turns back — all for a chart the Lab had already decided was not a stage.

Because that chart opens 3.2 hours before stage 1, it also dragged the whole time axis back with it, which is what made the well look like it had a stage split off on its own at the left-hand edge.

FracView now sees exactly the stages the list and the CSV do. On that file: 28 stages in all three, no nameless band, and no clock-turns-back banner. The Lab's own "stage starts before the stage before it finished" warning stops counting held-back charts for the same reason — a job-length re-plot opens before stage 1 by construction, and warning about a chart you cannot see helps nobody.

If you do want to look at it, Show under the stage list brings it back everywhere at once.
